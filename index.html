How to play
  1. Each card is a real company's results day, with the name hidden.
  2. Read what investors expected, what the company reported and what else it announced.
  3. Call it: did the stock go up or down the next day? Was the move big (5%+) or small?
  4. Bet 10%, 25% or 50% of your portfolio. Both calls right: you win your bet. Right direction, wrong size: you win half. Wrong direction: you lose it.
  5. The catch: good news doesn't always mean up. Prices move on surprise, not on whether the news sounds good.
